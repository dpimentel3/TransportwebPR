Frequently asked questions

Clear answers so you feel comfortable booking your ride.

Are your drivers and Captains licensed?
Yes. Our drivers/Captains are licensed professionals with years of experience in the business.
Do you provide safe and on-time transportation?
Yes. We focus on safe, reliable, and on-time transportation so visitors can travel with confidence.
Are your drivers, Tour Guides and Captains bilingual?
Yes. Our Drivers/Tour Guides/Captains are bilingual, making communication easier for both local and international travelers.
Do you offer group transportation?
Yes. We offer transportation for groups as well as private rides for individuals, couples, and families.
Do you operate every day?
Yes. Drivers operate 24/7, 365 days a year, and the business is available daily to help arrange transportation requests.
What information do I need to book?
Please provide the date, pickup time and location, destination, number of passengers, your first name, your phone number and email if necessary. After that, we will contact you and you must reply to confirm the trip.

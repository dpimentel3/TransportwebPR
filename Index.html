Cave Swimming Adventure

Vega Baja, PR

$95 / Person 4 Hours Min. 2 People Ages 5 up to 80

Dive into one of Puerto Rico's most thrilling mountain adventures with a cave swimming experience surrounded by rugged scenery, crystal-clear waters, and unforgettable natural beauty.

View Details
Experience Experience one of Puerto Rico's hidden gems with our River Cave Swimming Adventure in the mountains of Vega Baja. Your experience includes round-trip transportation, so you can relax while we handle everything from start to finish. Once in Vega Baja, you'll hike through lush mountain terrain and swim through stunning natural caves—an unforgettable, off-the-beaten-path adventure. This is an intermediate-level activity perfect for those looking for a mix of excitement and exploration. We provide life jackets and bottled water for your safety and comfort. What to bring: sneakers (for hiking), a towel, and a change of clothes for after the tour. Tour Length The experience lasts approximately 4 hours, and we never rush—your designated driver stays with you, ensuring you can fully enjoy every moment at your own pace.

Yes. We offer transportation for groups as well as private rides for individuals, couples, and families.

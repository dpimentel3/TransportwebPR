Snorkel Through Crystal-Clear Waters With Puerto Rico’s Marine Life

Snorkel With Turtles

San Juan, PR

$39 / Person 1 to 2 Hours Min. 2 person Ages 8+

Explore the crystal-clear waters of San Juan, Puerto Rico on this beginner-friendly snorkeling adventure. Accompanied by experienced local guides, you'll visit beautiful coral reef ecosystems teeming with tropical marine life. Swim through calm, pristine waters and keep an eye out for majestic sea turtles in their natural habitat. With no previous snorkeling experience required, this tour is perfect for families, first-time snorkelers, and anyone looking to experience Puerto Rico's underwater beauty.

Experience

Your snorkeling adventure begins with a brief introduction on the beach, where your professional guide will cover the fundamentals of snorkeling and ensure you feel comfortable with the equipment. You'll then move into shallow, waist-deep water to practice basic techniques and build confidence at your own pace. Once you're ready, your guide will lead you on a memorable snorkeling tour through the crystal-clear waters, pointing out colorful fish, marine life, and other underwater highlights while capturing free of charge photos and videos so you can relive the experience long after the tour ends. Meeting Point: Escambron Beach, San Juan, Puerto Rico. Uber is recommended for this tour. Arrive at least 30 minutes before hand.

Tour Length

Can last up to 2 hours, transport can be booked on the transportation page.
